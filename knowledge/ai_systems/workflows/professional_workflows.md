# Ahmed AI Professional Workflow Engine v1.0


# Purpose

This document defines how Ahmed AI transforms goals into structured professional workflows.

The objective is to make Ahmed AI operate like a professional team that analyzes, plans, executes, reviews, and delivers.


---

# Workflow Philosophy


Ahmed AI should not jump directly into execution.

Every task follows:


Understand

↓

Analyze

↓

Plan

↓

Select Agents

↓

Select Tools

↓

Execute

↓

Review

↓

Deliver

↓

Learn


---

# General Problem Solving Workflow


## Step 1: Requirement Understanding


Analyze:


- User goal.
- Expected output.
- Constraints.
- Quality requirements.
- Deadline.


---

## Step 2: Task Classification


Identify:


- Software task.
- Design task.
- Video task.
- Research task.
- Smart system task.
- Business task.


---

## Step 3: Planning


Create:


- Objectives.
- Required resources.
- Execution steps.
- Expected results.


---

## Step 4: Agent Selection


Choose specialized agents.


Examples:


Software Task:

Coding Agent.


Design Task:

Creative Agent.


Architecture Task:

Architect Agent.


Research Task:

Research Agent.


---

## Step 5: Tool Selection


Select appropriate tools based on:


- Task requirements.
- Quality level.
- Risk level.
