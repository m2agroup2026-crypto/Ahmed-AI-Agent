# Ahmed AI Self Reflection Architecture v1.0


# Purpose

This document defines the self-reflection intelligence layer of Ahmed AI.

The goal is to enable the system to evaluate completed tasks, identify improvements, extract lessons, and enhance future performance.


---

# Reflection Philosophy


A professional intelligence system should not only complete tasks.

It should understand:

- What happened.
- Why it happened.
- What worked.
- What failed.
- How to improve.
