# NEXORA AI Market Entry Strategy v1.0


# Goal

Define the strategy for launching NEXORA AI as a scalable AI SaaS platform.


---

# Entry Philosophy

NEXORA AI enters the market by solving clear business problems with accessible AI solutions.

The focus:

Problem first.

Customer value first.

Simple adoption.


---

# First Product


## NEXORA Business AI


An AI operating assistant for small and medium businesses.


Purpose:

Help businesses organize knowledge, automate communication, and improve daily operations.


---

# Initial Target Market


Primary users:


- Small businesses.
- Clinics.
- Service companies.
- Retail businesses.
- Professional offices.


Reason:

High repetitive tasks and clear operational problems.


---
