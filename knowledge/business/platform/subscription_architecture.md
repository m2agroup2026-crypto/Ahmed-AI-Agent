# NEXORA AI Subscription Architecture v1.0


# Purpose


Define the subscription and monetization system of NEXORA AI SaaS platform.


The goal:

Provide accessible AI services while maintaining scalable business operations.


---


# Subscription Philosophy


NEXORA AI uses a flexible subscription model.


The model combines:


Subscription Plans

+

Usage Limits

+

AI Credits


---


# Subscription Plans


## Free Plan


Purpose:

Allow users to experience NEXORA AI.


Features:


- Basic AI access.
- Limited conversations.
- Basic agents.
- Limited memory.
- Limited workspace resources.


---


## Starter Plan


Purpose:

Support individual professionals and small users.


Features:


- Higher usage limits.
- More AI agents.
- Larger memory.
- File processing.


---


## Professional Plan


Purpose:

Support advanced users.


Features:


- Advanced agents.
- Automation workflows.
- Larger knowledge base.
- Priority processing.


---


## Enterprise Plan


Purpose:

Support organizations.


Features:


- Organization workspace.
- Team management.
- Custom AI agents.
- Private knowledge base.
- Advanced permissions.
- Enterprise support.


---


# Credit System


Some AI operations require additional resources.


Credits may be used for:


- Large document analysis.
- Advanced AI operations.
- Image generation.
- Video processing.
- Specialized agents.


---


# Usage Tracking


NEXORA tracks:


- Messages.
- AI operations.
- Storage usage.
- Agent executions.
- Workflow executions.
