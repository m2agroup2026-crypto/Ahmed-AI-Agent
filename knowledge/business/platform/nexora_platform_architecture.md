# NEXORA AI Platform Architecture v1.0


# Vision


NEXORA AI is designed as a scalable AI platform that provides intelligent systems for individuals, professionals, and organizations.


The platform combines:

- AI Agents
- Knowledge Systems
- Memory
- Decision Intelligence
- Automation
- Enterprise Workspaces


---


# Platform Architecture


NEXORA AI consists of multiple layers:


# 1. User Layer


Purpose:

Provide access to AI capabilities.


Components:

- User Accounts
- Authentication
- User Profiles
- Personal Workspaces


---


# 2. AI Intelligence Core


Purpose:

Provide the intelligence engine.


Components:


## Agents

Specialized AI workers.


## Tools

Controlled execution capabilities.


## Memory

Experience storage and retrieval.


## Judgment

Decision analysis and recommendations.


## Reflection

Continuous improvement through evaluation.


---


# 3. Business Platform Layer


Purpose:

Transform AI capabilities into a SaaS product.


Components:


## Organizations

Allow companies to create dedicated AI environments.


## Teams

Manage multiple users inside organizations.


## Subscriptions

Support different pricing plans.


## Billing

Handle payments and invoices.


## Usage Tracking

Measure AI consumption.


## Permissions

Control access levels.


---


# 4. Enterprise Brain Layer


Purpose:

Create a digital intelligence system for each organization.


Components:


## Company Knowledge

Business documents and information.


## Custom Agents

Industry-specific AI specialists.


## Workflows

Automated business processes.


## Business Memory

Store company experience and decisions.


---


# 5. Security Architecture


Requirements:


- User data isolation.
- Organization isolation.
- Permission control.
- Secure data handling.
- Audit logging.


---


# 6. Subscription Model


NEXORA AI supports:


## Free

Basic experience.


## Plus

Advanced personal usage.


## Professional

Power users and creators.


## Enterprise

Organizations with custom AI systems.

# 7. Future Expansion


The platform can support:

# Long Term Goal


NEXORA AI becomes an intelligence operating layer for organizations.
